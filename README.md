**Built an Amazon Sales Dashboard in Excel (2023–2024)**

I turned raw Amazon order data into a one-page dashboard that answers the questions a business team actually asks: what are we selling, where, how are customers paying, and where are we losing orders?

**Headline KPIs**

Total Revenue: 39.4M <br>
Total Orders: 39,966 <br>
Total Customers: 27,484 <br>
Average Order Value: ~985.90<br>
Cancellation Rate: 3% | Return Rate: 3%<br>

**What the dashboard covers**

Monthly trends, 2023 vs 2024<br>
Sales by category<br>
Payment mode split<br>
Order status breakdown<br>
Sales by country<br>
Top 10 products<br>

**Key insights**

🔹 The US is the biggest market. It brings in roughly 70% of revenue. India comes second, and Canada, the UK and Australia are much smaller.<br>
🔹 Cards lead payments. Credit card is the top payment mode, followed by debit card. Cash on Delivery is the smallest share.<br>
🔹 Categories are balanced. All six categories land in a similar range, with Electronics narrowly ahead, so there is no single-category dependence.<br>
🔹 Fulfilment is healthy. Most orders are delivered, with only ~3% cancelled and ~3% returned.<br>
🔹 Seasonality is visible. February is the weakest month in both years, and May 2024 is the peak.<br>
🔹 No single hero product. The top 10 products sit close together, with Dress Shirt at #1.<br>

**What I'd recommend**

Run promotions in the low-demand months (especially February) to smooth out revenue.<br>
Look at growing international markets so the business relies less on one country.<br>
